# Product Vision Document

## Product Name

**WhatsApp Commerce & Retention OS**

---

## What It Is

A SaaS platform that enables businesses to sell, support, and retain customers directly through their WhatsApp Business number.

Customers interact through WhatsApp, while businesses manage products, orders, customers, and retention campaigns from a unified dashboard.

---

## Problem Statement

Small and medium businesses struggle with:

- Customer retention
- Repeat purchases
- Manual order management
- Scattered customer data
- Slow customer support
- Lack of automation
- Dependence on multiple disconnected tools

Most businesses focus on acquiring new customers while losing revenue from existing customers.

---

## Market Opportunity

WhatsApp is already the primary communication channel for millions of businesses and customers worldwide.

Businesses increasingly use WhatsApp for:

- Product inquiries
- Order placement
- Customer support
- Promotions
- Customer engagement

However, most businesses lack a complete system to manage and automate these interactions.
